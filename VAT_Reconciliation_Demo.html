Review boundaries¶

Examples preserve the earlier missing-net-value, source-only, customs and prior-period breakdowns. Amount differences are covered separately by tests. A match does not establish deductibility or completeness of supporting documents. The case page retains a reported manual baseline of approximately two hours and an estimated target of 15-20 minutes, pending validation. These are not measured savings.
